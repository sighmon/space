The JPEG2000 files carry their own polar-stereographic CRS (radius 1737.4 km, latitude of origin −90, central meridian 0). Their embedded upper-left corner is (-457440.0, 457440.0) m, which differs by (-120.0, 120.0) m. The CRS name string on those files says `Moon2000_npole` even though the projection parameters are the south pole. The parameters are what get used. The embedded grid is sampled. The label origin is not substituted for it.

Diviner's summer-maximum GeoTIFF measures 240.036 m per pixel against the published 240 m bin. That difference is inside the frame check, and the temperature is quoted at the measured spacing, not as a 5 m value.

Site 06's datum is named `unknown` inside the GeoTIFF. The spheroid radius is 1737400 m and the projection is polar stereographic with latitude of origin −90. That matches the frame. The product README at the same archive states the MOON_ME / DE421 axes for these files.

Horizon masks for the timed Sun and Earth series use Site06_final_adj_5mpp_surf.tif. That grid's own georeferencing is the one above. The illumination label origin is not used.

The nine-region comparison samples one published point on each additional product-78 tile. A point that falls outside a tile, or a region with no product-78 file, has no slope value from that file. Those tiles are not resampled onto 5 m from a coarser mosaic. Illumination and Diviner stay on the pixels measured above.
