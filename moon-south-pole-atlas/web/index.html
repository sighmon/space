Method and sources

What each readout means

Slope

Median and 5–95% range of slope over a 20 m baseline inside a 200 m patch, from a LOLA-derived DEM. Uncertainty combines DEM vertical error and baseline choice.

Sunlight

Average fraction of the solar disc above the local horizon, hourly over the stated window, at 1 m or 5 m above ground. Horizons from LOLA topography; Sun position from JPL DE421.

Earth view

Share of hours with any part of Earth’s disc above the local horizon (DE421 + LOLA horizons). Slightly optimistic versus a DSN link budget, which needs margin above the horizon.

Cold ground

Distance to the nearest ground whose modelled annual maximum temperature stays below 110 K (Diviner), with permanent shadow mapped from LOLA illumination. Proximity only: no ice-volume estimate.

What is real in this build

Only Nobile Rim 1 / NR101 is computed. Its slope, slope RMS, 18.6-year sunlight and Earth averages, Diviner temperature, distance to cold shadow, and the hourly Sun/Earth series (2025-01-01, 14 and 30 Earth days, 1 m and 5 m) are exported from the repository code to web/data/nr101.json. The other eight regions show placeholder values labelled example. The 3D-looking Moon is procedural art, not terrain. The slope and cold-ground notes above describe the example model; for NR101 see each readout's own line.
