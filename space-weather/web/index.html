Method and sources

Real, copied and illustrative

Real · flux, alerts, dose

GOES-13 flux samples, the SWPC alerts and warnings, and the CRaTER dose rate are read from the archived files below by the repository's own code and exported unchanged. Samples with a non-zero quality flag or no value are gaps.

Real · hours, first and last, peaks

Counted from the exported series at 5-minute samples: “at or above” the published threshold. First to last is a span, not a continuous stretch; the number of separate stretches is shown beside it.

Illustrative · hero halo and rings

The three audience rings (crew and polar fill with log₁₀ of the real flux against the published threshold marker; the grid ring is an 11-day dial of issued K≥7 alerts and the warning valid period, not observed K) and the procedural halo are drawings with real values. Radius and brightness follow log₁₀ of the real flux at the replay time; the rays are decoration. It is not an image of radiation or of the magnetosphere.

Illustrative · replay speed

1× is 3 hours of storm per second, 4× is 12, 16× is 48. The replay loops over the 11 days. No latency is measured: the data are archives.

How to read it

GOES-13 is a geostationary satellite. Its unit, p/(cm² s sr), is read as pfu only to compare with published thresholds. CRaTER flew on the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter in lunar orbit: its dose rate is not the dose at flight altitude, in a crew vehicle or on the ground, and it is copied from RadLab, not derived from flux. The two are shown side by side only. There are no error bars because none are published in these files. Published scales and definitions are shown with their source; they are not flight rules, aviation limits or grid limits.
